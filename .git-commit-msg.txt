Add optimization level controls for z88dk and cc65, plus status bar improvements

- z88dk: new optLevel option (0-3) maps to sccz80's -O<n> or sdcc's -SO<n>
  peephole optimizer flag, validated server-side and threaded through
  compileWithZ88dk into the zcc invocation.
- cc65: new optLevel option (O/Oi/Or/Os) maps directly to cl65's own
  optimization flags, validated server-side and threaded through
  compileWithCc65 into the cl65 invocation.
- Client: replaced the plain Compile button with a per-platform
  CompileControl slot on PlatformProvider. Both platforms now render a
  single split button (e.g. "Compile O2") with an attached caret that
  opens a menu to pick the optimization level - extracted into a shared
  SplitCompileButton component reused by both platforms' index.tsx.
- CompileResponse now includes the exact command line invoked (zcc/cl65 +
  args); StatusBar shows it at the end, click-to-copy with "Copied!"
  feedback, and it grows to fill the remaining status bar width instead
  of being capped at a fixed max-width.
- StatusBar condensed: byte/cycle subtotal is now "<Scope> <n>B/<n>T"
  with a plain-English hover tooltip ("Program: 38 bytes and 238
  cycles"), and the compile time label was dropped (just "123ms").
